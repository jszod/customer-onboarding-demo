Task 22 · Step 1 · approve before any CSS moves

The onboarding console, per §13.1

§13.1 was written from reading the stylesheet, not from looking at the page. This is what it produces. Both themes are shown at once and are forced — they ignore your system setting, so you are seeing the real pair rather than whichever one you happen to be in.

Two changes you have to agree to

Everything else in §13.1 is cleanup — collapsing duplicate values, fixing a breakpoint that disagreed with itself, adding focus rings that were never there. These two are visible design changes, and they are the reason this page exists.

Decision 1

All body copy gets one step heavier

body currently declares no weight, so unstyled text renders at 400. §13.1's three weights are 500 / 600 / 700, so body now declares 500 — every paragraph, note, sub-label and table cell gains a step. Intended: §13.1's first principle is scanned, not read. But it is the single most visible change in the task.

font-weight: 400 → 500
Decision 2

The current-stage title grows to 21px

.detail-title — the "what is happening right now" headline — was 19px. The six-size scale has no 19px step, so it either drops to 17px and joins the panel headings, or rises to 21px and matches the client name. It rises: on a projector this is the line the room is reading.

19px → 21px (--fs-display)
